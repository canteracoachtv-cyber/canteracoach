Cantera Coach Studio

Youth basketball training, shared with coaches.

Cantera Coach creates short training videos with drills and tips for coaches who work in youth basketball. Cantera Coach Studio is our internal tool to schedule and publish that content to our own social media accounts.

What Cantera Coach Studio does

Cantera Coach Studio is used only by the Cantera Coach team. It is not a public service and it does not publish content on behalf of other users.

Connect

The account owner signs in with TikTok to authorize Cantera Coach Studio for our own TikTok account.

Plan

Our training videos and captions are organized in a content calendar with a scheduled publishing date.

Publish

The creator reviews each video, caption and privacy settings and confirms before it is published to TikTok.
