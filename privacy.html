Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cantera Coach Studio (the "Service") handles personal data. The Service is an internal tool used by Cantera Coach to publish its own youth basketball training videos to its own TikTok account.

1. Data controller

Cantera Coach, Madrid, Spain. Contact: canteracoach.tv@gmail.com

2. Data we process

When the account owner connects a TikTok account through Login with TikTok, the Service receives and processes only:

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic): the TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used to publish content to the connected account.
  • Creator settings returned by TikTok (such as available privacy options), used to display the publishing options before each post.
  • Content we publish: our own videos, captions and publishing settings.

The Service does not collect data about TikTok viewers, followers or any other users, and does not use cookies or analytics.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Data is used solely to publish Cantera Coach's own content to its own TikTok account, at the request of the account owner. The legal basis is the account owner's consent, given when authorizing the Service with TikTok, and Cantera Coach's legitimate interest in managing its own accounts.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and publishing data are stored encrypted on a server controlled by Cantera Coach in Spain. Access is restricted to the Cantera Coach team.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok, as required to publish content through the TikTok API.

6. Retention

Tokens are kept only while the TikTok account remains connected. They are deleted when access is revoked or the account is disconnected from the Service.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability, by writing to canteracoach.tv@gmail.com. You can revoke the Service's access to your TikTok account at any time from your TikTok settings. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the latest update.
